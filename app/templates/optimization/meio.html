Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization
Methodology: risk-pooling approximation — downstream nodes hold safety stock only for the short internal replenishment lead time; the central node pools downstream demand variability (sqrt of summed variances) and absorbs the full external supplier lead-time uncertainty. This is a genuine trade-off, not a guaranteed win: pooling reduces safety stock driven by independent demand noise, but concentrates safety stock driven by shared supplier lead-time risk. The result reports whichever direction the numbers actually show. See docs/optimization.md for the full derivation and its limits.
