Reading the score

  • ≥ 80 Normal ✓ healthy
  • 65–80 Watch ◔ drifting
  • 50–65 Attention ▲ needs action
  • < 50 Critical ✖ intervene

Each component is a 0–100 linear score between a "worst" and "best" anchor (e.g. service level 80 %→0, 99 %→100). Components with no data are dropped, never guessed.
