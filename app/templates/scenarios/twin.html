Twin governance

  • Scenario ID, creator, base dataset, assumptions, changes, results and timestamp are stored for every run.
  • PairInputs are deep-copied before mutation; no ORM object is written during a simulation.

Simulation mechanics

  1. Arrivals for the step (existing inbound with sampled ETA delay, scenario delays, disruptions).
  2. Demand ~ N(μΔ, σ²Δ) with scenario multipliers (promotions, spikes).
  3. Sales = min(stock, demand); unmet demand is lost (share) or backordered.
  4. Review: if position ≤ s, order S − position (MOQ/multiple honoured); lead time sampled from the fitted lognormal.
  5. Costs: holding, expedite/transfer, lost margin, priced carbon; service, stock-out rate, working capital.

Steps: 1 day / 1 week / 1 month. Discrete-event style ordering within each step; time-phased at minimum.
