Order pegging & allocation

Inventory → sales order / production order / transfer → customer. Supplies (on-hand lots by FEFO/FIFO, POs, transfers, production) are pegged to demands ranked by the configured allocation policy. One supply can feed many demands and vice-versa; a unit is never double-committed and a demand is never over-allocated.
